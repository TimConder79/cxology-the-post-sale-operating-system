CXologyDesk

A new way to use a business book: bring a real customer situation, get the play to run, grounded in The Post-Sale Operating System and cited to the page.

This is an early prototype preview. Answers shown here are real outputs from testing, played back without a live connection.

1 · The Desk

Ask the book

A simple search box. Pick a sample situation and see the play card: what's really going on, the play to run, your next 3 moves, and how you'll know it's working, every claim cited.
2 · Action kits

From advice to action

A detailed renewal scenario where each move comes with a ready-to-use prompt for Claude or ChatGPT, plus downloadable templates built on the book's frameworks.
3 · Test results

How it handled real and edge cases

Every test answer side by side, including vague questions where it asked for detail and off-topic questions where it said plainly the book doesn't cover them.
