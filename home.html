The Post-Sale Gap

Before the sale, progression is designed. After the sale, we mostly hope for it.

Sales has a defined process. Marketing has one. Product has one. Then the contract gets signed, and too much of that discipline disappears, replaced by individual effort and instinct dressed up as process. Post-sale is not churn insurance. It is a revenue engine, and it deserves the same operational rigor.

The dread

The QBR with nothing new to say

A meeting that feels like a ritual instead of something that moved the customer forward.

The doubt

A health score you don't believe

Reported on, but built on lagging signals that move only after the problem already happened.

The silence

An account that stalled, and nobody could say why

Silence can look a lot like success. Until it isn't.

Four parts. One working system.
