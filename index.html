About Tim

I build systems that make retention predictable and growth repeatable.

Over 20+ years, I've worked at the intersection of Customer Success, Product, and Operations — starting as a software engineer and evolving into a CS executive responsible for revenue-critical relationships and global teams. Across every role, one belief has proven true: the best companies don't leave retention to chance — they design for it.

That belief was shaped most significantly during my time at Aprimo, where I led a global services organization supporting roughly 65% of a $100M revenue base. Over six consecutive years, we achieved 100% logo retention while maintaining strong growth and margins. That experience changed how I think about Customer Success — not as a function, but as a system.

CXology was built to codify what I learned — translating years of experience into a structured, scalable model for post-sale execution. The system is anchored by a post-sale pipeline: Identify → Align → Advocate → Intent → Net Revenue Close.

What I've found is that while every company invests heavily in a pre-sales pipeline — creating visibility, accountability, and continuous improvement for new revenue — the post-sale motion is often left unstructured. Retention and expansion are expected, but rarely managed with the same rigor.

The post-sale pipeline addresses this gap. While it differs from a traditional sales pipeline, the benefits are the same: structure, visibility, and the ability to continuously improve. It creates a shared way of working across Customer Success, Sales, and Product — ensuring that customer value is defined, stakeholders are aligned, and both risk and opportunity are surfaced early and managed intentionally.

But the pipeline alone isn't what drives results. The operating system comes to life through a set of integrated plays — repeatable processes embedded at key moments across the customer lifecycle. These plays are what ensure the system is consistently executed, turning strategy into action and action into measurable outcomes. Together, the pipeline and these integrated plays create the foundation for consistently driving Net Revenue Retention — moving beyond isolated efforts to a coordinated, scalable approach to growth.

This model reframes Customer Success from reactive account management into proactive, outcome-driven engagement. Recently, I made the decision to move from building CXology independently into a full-time leadership role. The opportunity is in embedding and operationalizing these systems within a company — where they can directly impact customers, teams, and revenue.
