Chapter 18

AI in CS: Judgment Over Templates

In 2026, AI is not optional. But deployed incorrectly, it can hollow out the value of your entire CS team.

We have reached a definitive turning point in the software industry. In 2026, Artificial Intelligence (AI) is no longer a futuristic buzzword or an optional upgrade; it is an absolute, non-negotiable requirement for any serious Customer Success organization. Teams that hesitated or delayed their AI adoption in 2025 are now scrambling to catch up, rapidly falling behind their AI-enabled peers.

However, as we rush to adopt these powerful tools, we must address a massive hidden risk. If deployed incorrectly, AI can severely damage the customer experience and hollow out the value of your Customer Success team.

  • The Trap of "Standardized" CS and Anti-Innovation There is a dangerous temptation to use AI to automate absolutely everything. When companies rely on AI to write every email, generate every strategy, and dictate every response, it risks becoming the ultimate "anti-innovation". It automates creativity and decision-making, stripping away the human fulfillment that comes from active problem-solving and forming an identity within the work.

Industry experts are loudly warning against the risk of AI "standardizing" CSMs. When an entire department relies on the exact same AI-generated templates and automated cadences, CSMs stop thinking critically. They become administrators of an automated system rather than strategic advisors. If your customer feels like they are talking to a robot reading a generic script, you will instantly lose the trust you worked so hard to build during the Kickoff and Onboarding phases.

  • Customer Success is Fundamentally Human To deploy AI correctly, we must remember a foundational truth: Customer Success is human.

At its core, CS is about people helping people. It is about empowering others to succeed in complex, nuanced ways that AI simply cannot replicate. An AI cannot look an executive sponsor in the eye and navigate the internal political friction of their organization. An AI cannot provide genuine empathy when a customer is frustrated by a missed commitment. You must preserve your team's human judgment, relationship-building skills, and critical thinking.

  • From Administrative Assistants to Autonomous "Agentic" Workflows The true power of AI in Customer Success is not in replacing your team's judgment; it is in removing their busywork and executing scalable workflows.

Historically, AI was utilized primarily for basic administrative tasks, but the industry trend for 2026 emphasizes generative AI moving from simple chat assistants to autonomous "agents" executing complex workflows. You should aggressively deploy AI to handle the heavy administrative lifting and scaled tech-touch engagements that drain your team's capacity. Modern CS organizations are successfully utilizing these agentic workflows for:

  • Health Scoring and Predictive Models: Processing massive amounts of usage data, support activity, and commercial signals to identify early churn risks and likely upsell candidates long before a human CSM could spot them.
  • Proactive Intelligence (Next Best Actions): AI acting as a proactive copilot, surfacing recommended next best actions by suggesting exactly which play to run, who to contact, and what message to use based on account context and history.
  • Automated Playbooks ("Motions"): Moving beyond AI that just surfaces insights. Modern agentic systems feature playbook automation that autonomously launches email sequences, routes tickets to the correct department, and triggers in-app campaigns when specific health conditions are met, closing the loop from insight to action without requiring manual triage.
  • 24/7 Digital-First Support: Implementing shared customer success hubs and virtual agents that provide continuous, always-on support to handle FAQs and low-complexity configuration issues, automatically escalating nuanced topics to a human CSM with full context.
  • Data Entry and Admin Automators: Automatically capturing meeting notes, summarizing calls, extracting action items, and logging the answers to the Five Questions, ensuring your "Single Source of Truth" is always accurate without requiring hours of manual data entry.
  • Judgment Over Templates When you use autonomous agentic workflows to eliminate the administrative burden and handle low-complexity support, you free up your CSMs to do what they do best: act as strategic advisors who understand the customer's business case and talk ROI with executives.

The guiding principle for your team in 2026 must be Judgment Over Templates. Let the AI agent write the first draft of the Sharing Insights email. Let AI aggregate the data for the Alignment Meeting. Let AI flag the accounts that are stalling in their Goal Blocks and automatically trigger a tech-touch nudge. But when dealing with high-value accounts, complex escalations, or nuanced renewals, a human CSM must apply their critical thinking and judgment to handle the exceptions and tailor the strategy.

When you strike this balance, AI ceases to be a threat to your relationships. Instead, it becomes the ultimate lever for scale, allowing your team to deliver highly personalized, deeply human value to every single customer in your portfolio.

AI in the System

The mistake most companies make with AI is thinking about it as a tool. A better way to think about it is as an execution layer that runs alongside your Post-Sale Operating System.

AI is not something you “use” occasionally. It is something that operates continuously, shaping how your plays are executed, how signals are detected, and how actions are triggered across the customer lifecycle.

In the Purchase & Welcome Play, AI accelerates the first impression. It drafts personalized welcome messages, summarizes the internal knowledge transfer, and ensures that no customer experiences silence in the critical moments after purchase.

In the Kickoff Play, AI prepares the CSM before the meeting ever begins. It synthesizes account context, surfaces risks and opportunities, and ensures the conversation starts at the right level—focused on outcomes, not information gathering.

During Onboarding and First Value, AI monitors progress in real time. It identifies where customers are stalling, triggers the right interventions, and helps teams move from reactive support to proactive guidance.

In ongoing plays like Alignment Meetings and Sharing Insights, AI becomes a co-pilot. It aggregates data, identifies patterns, and suggests the next best actions, allowing the CSM to focus on interpretation, storytelling, and strategy.

And in Renew & Grow, AI connects everything. It highlights expansion signals, flags risk early, and ensures that renewal conversations are grounded in value, not urgency.

AI does not replace the system. It runs the system at scale.

When AI is embedded this way, the principle of Judgment Over Templates becomes real. The system handles the repetition. The human handles the decision.
